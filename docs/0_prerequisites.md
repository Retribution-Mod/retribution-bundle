# Prerequisites

Before installing Retribution, make sure you meet the requirements for your platform.

## Android

- A compatible Android device running Android 8 (Oreo) or newer.
- The official Discord app installed (the manager can also download and install it for you).
- One of the following loaders:
  - **Root / Xposed users**: a working Xposed or LSPosed installation.
  - **Non-root users**: the [Retribution Manager](https://github.com/Retribution-Mod/retribution-manager/releases/latest).

## iOS

- An iPhone or iPad running a compatible iOS version.
- One of the following installation methods:
  - **Jailbreak / TrollStore**: a jailbroken device or TrollStore installed.
  - **Sideloading**: a sideloading tool such as AltStore, SideStore, Scarlet, or a signing service.

> **Note:** Sideloaded iOS builds require a *decrypted* Discord IPA. Retribution does not provide Discord IPAs; you must supply your own.

## General

- A stable internet connection so the loader can download the latest `retribution-new.min.js` or `retribution-old.min.js` bundle, depending on your Discord build.
- Enough free storage for the patched or sideloaded Discord app.
